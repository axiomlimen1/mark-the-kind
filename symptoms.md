# Three symptoms, three moves

For the human at the keyboard, after something has already come back wrong.
`publisher-card.md` is what you do before pressing publish. This page is for the
minute after. Find the symptom you are looking at, make the one move, stop.

| What you are looking at | What it actually is | Your move |
|---|---|---|
| `自动验证异常，请重新提交` (automatic verification exception, please resubmit) | The checker failed. The post did not. First seen 2026-11-01; again 2026-11-03, third time on the same claim, same exception, marker still exactly `@grovetravel`. | Resubmit the identical string, unchanged, with the timestamped screenshot attached. Touch no field. A field that was right becomes a real mismatch the moment it is edited under pressure. |
| The task closes (`task_closed`, or "closed") with no revision, no verdict, and the clock still running | Nobody reviewed it. A correct submission was swallowed by the queue, not by the post. 2026-11-01, reported by @Celia Rose. | Nothing on the post repairs this. Keep the link and the timestamped screenshot, give the agent the exact state word, and stop editing. |
| The link plays for you but asks a stranger to log in, or says the video is unavailable | Not public. The reviewer's tool cannot open it. Two claims lost this way on 2026-10-31 with every marker correct. | Do not submit it. Fix the visibility setting, copy the link again from the post's own Share button, and re-test in a private window or a phone that has never signed in. |

---

If what you see is not in this table, do not guess at a fix. Send the exact error
text, in its own language, to the agent and to nobody else. Guessing is how a
correct field turns into a real mismatch.

Every row has a date and a named source. A symptom without a date is a rumour,
and a rumour is not a reason to edit anything.

Written 2026-11-05 after @Landon read `publisher-card.md` and asked for the
verifier exception to have its own page: "Resubmit unchanged" is the correct
move and almost nobody makes it.
